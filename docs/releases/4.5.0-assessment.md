## Performance Evidence And Compatibility

The [September 9 benchmark](4.5.0-performance.md) compares explicit source
revisions with controlled dependencies; it is not a complete release-install
comparison or a fresh measurement of `bc0de261`.

- [4939603](https://github.com/emulsify-ds/emulsify-core/commit/4939603d05ca8b233aa49f60844782e455bb03bd)
  lazily constructs grouped candidates. Repeated early lookups showed 57–70%
  lower median time in two seven-sample batches, with identical outcomes and
  filesystem probes. Late/missing lookups and whole audits showed no consistent
  improvement. At those measured revisions, directory discovery was complete
  and cached by the caller; containment, ordering, and invalidation were
  unchanged by the measured optimization. The September 11 discovery and HMR
  corrections were not part of those measurements.
- The more precise 800-file story audit took longer and corrected findings;
  additional tests and AST parsing are not presented as a speed improvement.
- Accessibility cap two took about 45% longer than unbounded scanning on the
  eight-story fixture and reduced sampled browser-process-tree RSS by about
  74%. RSS sampling has documented limits and is not Node-only memory or a
  precise peak measurement. The cap is a resource tradeoff.

No Storybook startup or production-build speedup is established. The
[Twig/PHP corpus](../twig-php-parity.md) preserves exact observed strings and
browser-equivalence distinctions; it neither harmonizes output nor promises
an official Core/Tools pairing. PHP remains optional.
